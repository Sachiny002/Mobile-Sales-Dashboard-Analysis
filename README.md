
# 📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview
This project analyzes mobile sales data using Power BI to generate business insights related to sales performance, customer ratings, transactions, brands, payment methods, and regional sales distribution.

The dashboard helps businesses understand:
- Sales trends
- Customer behavior
- Best-selling brands and models
- Payment preferences
- City-wise performance

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze total sales and quantity sold
- Identify top-performing brands and mobile models
- Compare payment method transactions
- Understand monthly and daily sales trends
- Visualize sales by city using maps

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- Total Sales: 769M
- Total Quantity: 19K
- Transactions: 4K
- Average Sales: 40K

### Visualizations
- Total Sales by City (Map)
- Monthly Quantity Trend
- Customer Ratings Funnel
- Transactions by Payment Method
- Brand-wise Sales Table
- Sales by Mobile Model
- Sales by Day Name

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Top Performing Brand
Apple generated the highest sales among all brands.

### 2. Best Payment Method
UPI contributed the highest percentage of transactions.

### 3. Peak Sales Month
July recorded the highest total quantity sold.

### 4. Customer Ratings
Most customers provided 5-star ratings.

### 5. High Sales Cities
Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Lucknow showed strong sales performance.
